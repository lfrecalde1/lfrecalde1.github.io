  RESEARCH FOCUS
I am a research engineer and PhD candidate in Robotics Engineering at Worcester Polytechnic Institute. I hold an MSc in
Control Systems Engineering and a bachelor’s degree in Mechatronics Engineering. My research aims to enable fully
onboard autonomous navigation in unstructured environments. I develop control and estimation algorithms using
nonlinear control theory, differential geometry, and numerical optimization. I take these methods from
dynamical models and physics-based simulation to real-world experiments.

  TECHNICAL SKILLS
Optimization
• Model predictive control (MPC): Nonlinear model predictive control (NMPC) and model predictive contouring
  control (MPCC); solution methods include sequential quadratic programming (SQP), SQP with real-time iteration
  (SQP-RTI), interior-point methods, differential dynamic programming (DDP), and iterative linear quadratic regulator
  (iLQR).
• Nonsmooth and hybrid optimal control: complementarity-constrained formulations and relaxation methods for
  trajectory optimization across hybrid mode transitions.
• Manifold optimization: Geometric formulations for robot pose estimation and control.
• Differentiable optimization: Implicit Karush–Kuhn–Tucker (KKT) differentiation to compute solution sensitivities
  for gradient-based MPC tuning and learning.
• Distributed estimation and control: Alternating direction method of multipliers (ADMM) for distributed sensor
  fusion and multi-robot coordination.
Modeling and Control
• Geometric control on Lie groups: Controller design and stability analysis for attitude and pose tracking.
• Task-priority control: Null-space projection and affine-subspace methods for prioritizing robotic tasks (unmanned
  aerial vehicle (UAV) inspection; tactile manipulation).
• Safety-critical control: CLFs and CBFs for robotic systems.
• Data-driven modeling and control: Dynamic mode decomposition with control (DMDc) and Koopman operator
  approximations for robot dynamics identification and predictive modeling.
