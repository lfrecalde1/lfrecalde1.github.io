Technical precision in the longer source CV
- At Luis's request, both firmware and onboard controller frequency figures were removed. The experience bullet now reads: “Implemented INDI body-rate control and a communication protocol in Betaflight.” Firmware code and timing logs were not audited.
- Unit dual quaternions represent rigid poses with constraints and a sign ambiguity. Avoid calling this a minimal or unique global pose parameterization, or suggesting it introduces physical coupling absent from conventional rigid-body dynamics.
- CLF/CBF guarantees depend on assumptions, feasibility, implementation, and hybrid-transition treatment. “Experimentally evaluated” is appropriate for a short CV.
- KKT-based sensitivities require regularity; complementarity and switching can make solutions nonsmooth. Your original wording names a valid technique but does not independently demonstrate expertise.
- iLQR and DDP are related, not identical algorithms; SQP-RTI is a particular real-time iteration scheme. ADMM does not automatically give global convergence for nonconvex robotic problems.
- Inertial fusion, contracting observers, differentiable MPC, ADMM, complementarity methods, and the broader simulator/tool list remain supplied skills; the selected papers do not independently substantiate every skill or implementation. The invariant/equivariant filtering bullet has been removed at Luis’s request.
