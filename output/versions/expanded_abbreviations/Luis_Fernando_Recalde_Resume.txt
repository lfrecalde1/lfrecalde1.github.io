Research Focus
I am a research engineer with a Master of Science in Control Systems Engineering and a bachelor’s degree in Mechatronics
Engineering. My research aims to enable fully onboard autonomous navigation in unstructured environments. I
pursue this goal by developing control and estimation algorithms using nonlinear control theory, differential
geometry, and numerical optimization. My work spans dynamical modeling, physics-based simulation, and
real-world experiments.

Technical Skills
Optimization
 • Model predictive control (MPC): Nonlinear model predictive control (NMPC) and model predictive contouring
   control (MPCC); sequential quadratic programming (SQP) and its real-time iteration scheme (SQP-RTI), interior-point
   methods, differential dynamic programming (DDP), and iterative linear quadratic regulator (iLQR).
 • Nonsmooth and hybrid optimal control: complementarity-constrained formulations and relaxation methods for
   trajectory optimization across mode transitions.
 • Manifold optimization: Geometric formulations for robot pose estimation and control.
 • Differentiable optimization: Implicit differentiation of Karush–Kuhn–Tucker (KKT) conditions to compute solution
   sensitivities for gradient-based MPC tuning and learning.
 • Distributed estimation and control: Alternating direction method of multipliers (ADMM) for distributed sensor
   fusion and multi-robot coordination.
Modeling and Control
 • Geometric control on Lie groups: Controller design and stability analysis for attitude and pose tracking.
 • Task-priority control: Null-space projection and affine-subspace methods for prioritizing robotic tasks (unmanned
   aerial vehicle (UAV) inspection; tactile manipulation).
 • Safety-critical control: Control Lyapunov functions (CLFs) and control barrier functions (CBFs) for robotic systems.
 • Data-driven modeling and control: Dynamic mode decomposition with control (DMDc) and Koopman operator
   approximations for robot dynamics identification and predictive modeling.
